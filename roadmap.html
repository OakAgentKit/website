Oak roadmap

Follow the trail

Direction, not delivery dates. Priorities may change with testing and feedback.

01 · Done

On the Trail (Shipped)

  • ✓ Done: One installable MCP, public docs and website.
  • ✓ Done: Tools across Robinhood Chain projects behind one local wallet.
  • ✓ Done: Free access with chat approvals and an optional daily spending limit.

02

Free Access and Burn Flywheel

  • Keep Oak free to use, funded by swap, bridge and partner referral fees.
  • Use fee income to buy back and burn $OAK on Robinhood Chain.
  • Test every release against live market prices before it ships.

03

Wider Reach

  • Pursue eligible MCP registry and official platform listings.
  • Share practical demos, tutorials and product updates.
  • Build working relationships with ecosystem teams.

04

Oak's Own Agents

  • Launch Oak's own agents, funded by Oak's income and using Oak's tools on Robinhood Chain.
  • Route the results of Oak's agents into additional $OAK buybacks and burns.
  • Expand examples, project recipes and integration guides for builders.

05

The Frontier

  • Explore later ideas such as agent access and cross-project workflows.
  • Research tools that connect useful workflows across Robinhood Chain projects.
  • Test automated strategies within defined spending limits and chat approvals.

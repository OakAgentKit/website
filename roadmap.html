Oak roadmap

Follow the trail

Direction, not delivery dates. Priorities may change with testing and feedback.

01 · Done

On the Trail (Shipped)

  • ✓ Done: One installable MCP, public docs and website.
  • ✓ Done: 700 tools across 31 Robinhood Chain projects.
  • ✓ Done: LayerBank and Pendle join the Robinhood Chain projects.

02

Public Launch (v0.2.0)

  • $OAK, subscriptions and public on-chain actions are available together in Oak 0.2.6, subject to plan phase and access checks.
  • Make transaction reviews and wallet approvals clearer and safer.
  • Test every release against live market prices before it ships.

03

Wider Reach

  • Pursue eligible MCP registry and official platform listings.
  • Share practical demos, tutorials and product updates.
  • Build working relationships with ecosystem teams.

04

Builder Expansion

  • Add Robinhood Chain projects and deepen existing integrations.
  • Expand examples, project recipes and integration guides.
  • Build Oak's own trading agent for Robinhood Chain: bring any AI model or API provider to power it.

05

The Frontier

  • Explore further $OAK fee utility.
  • Research tools that connect useful workflows across projects.
  • Test automated strategies and agentic finance within defined budgets and approvals.

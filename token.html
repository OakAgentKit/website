$OAK is live on Pons. Oak is free, and holding $OAK has no access perks. Oak uses 25% of its income to buy back and burn $OAK.

What holding $OAK means

Holding $OAK has no access perks. Every Oak tool works the same whether or not you hold $OAK. Its one link to Oak is the burns below.

Where the income comes from

Oak takes a 0.25% fee on swaps and bridges, plus partner referral fees. Each summary shows the fee before you say yes.

What a burn does

A burn permanently removes $OAK from the total supply. Oak makes its burns by hand, at least once a month.

Reads and actions

Read tools and actions across the 34 projects work for everyone. Actions need a verified ERC-8004 agent ID and your yes or no in chat.

How a burn works

  1. Oak sets aside 25% of its income. This is done by hand, at least once a month.
  2. Oak converts that income to USDG and sends the USDG to the OakBurner contract.
  3. An Oak operator wallet then calls buyAndBurn. It buys $OAK with that USDG at the current pool price, in small steps. No single buy is larger than 1/200 of the USDG liquidity the price oracle reports for the pool.
  4. Every $OAK it buys is burned, which permanently reduces the total supply.

The treasury and contract admin is the single-owner wallet 0x856bb126188bDB5e425D3ac9D59E9ffBD12e6ECf. One owner key can spend treasury funds and replace the price signer. 2. How Oak Takes Income

Read token mechanics

Oak is free to use. Read tools work immediately; action tools require a verified ERC-8004 identity and a yes in chat (except gloam_claim, which needs no identity). Legacy v1 and v2 billing contracts remain deployed on-chain and unused.

Oak takes a 0.25% fee on swaps and bridges, plus partner referral fees:

  1. 0.25% swap and bridge fee (25 basis points) to treasury 0x856bb126188bDB5e425D3ac9D59E9ffBD12e6ECf:
    • KyberSwap (kyberswap_quote, kyberswap_swap, and generic quote/swap when KyberSwap wins): feeAmount = "25", isInBps = true, chargeFeeBy = "currency_in", feeReceiver = 0x856bb126188bDB5e425D3ac9D59E9ffBD12e6ECf.
    • Velora (velora_quote, velora_swap, and generic quote/swap when Velora wins): partnerFeeBps = 25, partnerAddress = 0x856bb126188bDB5e425D3ac9D59E9ffBD12e6ECf, takeSurplus = false.
    • Relay (relay_quote, relay_bridge_in, relay_bridge_out): appFees = [{ recipient: "0x856bb126188bDB5e425D3ac9D59E9ffBD12e6ECf", fee: "25" }]. Relay collects the app fee off-chain into the app balance, claimable via relay_app_fee_claims; it need not appear as a treasury transfer in the deposit calldata. Relay also charges its own fees.
    • Pons launch-curve trades and direct Uniswap fallback routes: 0 bps from Oak; venue fees still apply.
  2. hood.ag referral fees:
    • On registerMany, registerManyUSDG, buy, and buyUSDG, Oak passes treasury 0x856bb126188bDB5e425D3ac9D59E9ffBD12e6ECf as referrer. Oak receives 5% of registrations and 4% of resales, and buyers get 5% and 4% off.
    • When the payer or seller is the treasury wallet itself (0x856bb126188bDB5e425D3ac9D59E9ffBD12e6ECf), referrer is 0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000000 because hood.ag's contracts reject self-referral (SelfReferral), so no referral share or discount applies.
  3. Borro Bookrunners referral share:
    • Borro is an official Oak partner. Borro's own team runs its product and contracts; Oak didn't build them and doesn't run them.
    • When a vault is listed on BorroBuilders (0x082Ae609bdC7ccEec0C58B2361a21c49dc2aE291) and the oak code is registered, borro_deposit routes through BorroBuilders.deposit(vault, assets, code, minShares) with code oak and minShares enforced on-chain (You receive at least {minShares} vault shares, or the deposit will not go through.), falling back to direct vault.deposit when unlisted or unregistered. Withdrawals target the vault directly.
    • Borro keeps 10% of the interest as its fee; on deposits using Oak's code, Borro pays Oak all of that fee for the first 12 months, then half, paid monthly in USDG (0x5fc5360d0400a0fd4f2af552add042d716f1d168, 6 decimals).

3. Price Source and the 50% Guard

Read token mechanics

Two-leg price calculation and on-chain checks

  • OAK/ETH leg (signed note): Because Pons V2 graduated pools use a fixed Uniswap v4 hook that records no on-chain TWAP observations, the Oak server's note issuer computes the ETH-volume-weighted mean of per-trade oakPerEthE18 over [now - 1800s, now), floor-rounded, and signs a signed 30-minute $OAK price note (PriceNote { wallet, oakPerEthE18, timestamp, nonce }). Each note has a 5-minute life (300 seconds), works once, and is bound to one caller wallet (msg.sender) and one consuming contract. The issuer refuses to sign if the pool is younger than 1,800 seconds, has fewer than 10 trades in the window, has under $5,000 two-sided depth, or lies outside the guard band.
  • ETH/USD leg (on-chain): ETH/USD is read on-chain by OakOracle.ethUsdE18() from the 30-minute average (observe([1800, 0])) of the ETH/USDG pool (0x52e65B17fB6E5BA00Ed806f37Afcd2DaA50271Ca). "USD" is the USDG quote of the on-chain ETH/USDG pool; Oak assumes USDG trades at a dollar.
  • 50% guard and 10% burner spot check: OakOracle enforces a 50% guard, rejecting any note whose oakPerEthE18 is not within 50% of the live pool price (spot / 2 <= note <= spot * 3 / 2, read live from PoolManager.extsload slot 6). OakBurner (0x660b86c5a3f9E1278cB5F01763fd0a630221C18B) also enforces a 10% spot check (spot * 9 / 10 <= note <= spot * 11 / 10).
  • Signer boundary: The price signer is the one always-on key in Oak: it is sign-only, holds no ETH, and is separate from the treasury owner key. The Treasury owner can replace the price signer (setPriceSigner), and setting the zero address pauses pricing. A dishonest or stolen signer alone is bounded by the oracle's guard to 0.5x to 1.5x of the live pool price, and OakBurner's 10% spot check narrows that further.

4. The 25% Buyback-and-Burn Sequence and OakBurner

Read token mechanics

25% of Oak's income buys back and burns $OAK.

The burn sequence

  1. Convert 25% of fee income to USDG: At least monthly, convert 25% of the period's fee income to USDG (0x5fc5360d0400a0fd4f2af552add042d716f1d168).
  2. Transfer USDG to OakBurner first: Transfer that USDG to OakBurner (0x660b86c5a3f9E1278cB5F01763fd0a630221C18B) before calling the burn (buyAndBurn spends USDG already held by the contract; it does not pull from the caller).
  3. Fetch a caller-bound burner price note: An operator wallet with an Oak session fetches a for: "burner" price note from POST /v1/price-note bound to that caller wallet (msg.sender, not address(0)).
  4. Call buyAndBurn in bounded chunks: The same wallet calls buyAndBurn(usdgIn, note, sig) in chunks of at most oracle.liquidityUsdgE6() / 200 (0.5% of live pool liquidity per call) along the fixed route USDG -> ETH -> $OAK, requiring minOakOut at a price limit of at most note * 1.02 (never the live spot price). Purchased $OAK is destroyed in the same transaction via burn(uint256), permanently reducing totalSupply().
  5. Public reporting: Burn posts say "we burned".
  6. Armed 90-day sweep timer: The single-owner treasury wallet can sweep stuck USDG from OakBurner to the treasury only after a 90-day timer started by a public transaction (armSweep, which records armedAt and armedBalance). This power is not limited to a broken burner. A successful burn cancels the timer only when its USDG input is at least the smaller of 1% of the armed balance (rounded up) and the most one burn call may take; a smaller burn succeeds but leaves the timer running. Calling sweepStuck() after 90 days moves at most the balance recorded when the timer was armed (min(balance, armedBalance)), so later deposits require a fresh 90-day timer.

5. $OAK Trading Fees to the Launch Wallet

Read token mechanics
  • Every $OAK trade on Pons V2 pays a 2% fee: 0.3% to Pons and 1.7% creator fee (0.7% standard creator share + 1% creator tax set at launch; venue buybacks are off).
  • All 1.7% goes to the launch wallet (0x84b1B9e5D1cAfD9543E684BB39E9cbD478189EE2), which is the Pons creator-fee recipient and manually claims positive ETH balances from the Pons fee escrow (0xd3AFEB2a57f70eF218Aa82451c51B2fb0416Ac9e) by owner-signed transaction.
  • Pons keeper dependency: On graduated pools, launch-token-side fees reach the fee escrow only after Pons's own conversion keeper converts them to ETH (until then, sweepPoolFees reverts InternalSwapRequiresOperator(), so the launch wallet skips the hook sweep and claims already-credited escrow balances).
  • Product revenue is separate: Oak's 0.25% swap and bridge fees and partner referral fees go to treasury 0x856bb126188bDB5e425D3ac9D59E9ffBD12e6ECf, with 25% of income converted to USDG and sent to OakBurner (0x660b86c5a3f9E1278cB5F01763fd0a630221C18B) to buy back and burn $OAK. $OAK Pons trading fees go to the launch wallet.
  • External Pons owner override: The external Pons owner (0x263ed295dAFaE1d9AAdD6E56c4B6F9f38eE019Dd) can propose replacing the creator-fee recipient subject to a 3-day public timelock that Oak cannot cancel.

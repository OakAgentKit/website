Oak's offer for HEY Research Lab Verified Builders

30 free days of Oak, with no plan fee and no $OAK holding requirement, for any project HEY Research Lab lists as a Verified Builder, with or without a token. This offer is Oak's: HEY Research Lab does not issue codes, and this is not an endorsement by HEY Research Lab of Oak or of any token or trade. HEY Research Lab takes no fee from this offer, and it changes nothing on HEY Research Lab.

How to claim

  1. Open your project's page on HEY Research Lab.
  2. Send a direct message to @OakAgentKit from the official X account linked there, with your HEY Research Lab project link.
  3. We check the Verified Builder badge and that the account matches, then reply with your code.

Want one-on-one setup help? Say so in the same message.

How to redeem

Tell your agent “redeem my Oak code” and paste your code, or run:

oak redeem <code>

Details

One code per project

It works once and activates one wallet, and each wallet can hold one grant. Keep it safe.

Deadline: Dec 25, 2026

Redeem from the Oak 0.2.2 release until the end of 2026-12-25 UTC (before 2026-12-26T00:00:00Z). The 30 days start at redemption; if you already subscribe, they start when your paid time ends.

What you still pay

Network gas (including the one-time identity registration), each project's own fees and your own capital. Identity checks, confirmation forms, previews and local signing all stay on.

What you need first

A little ETH on Robinhood Chain for gas (your agent can bridge it in with relay_bridge_in) and an ERC-8004 identity: oak setup register, or oak setup link <agentId> for one you already own. Install steps are on the set up page.

After the 30 days

Paid features pause and Oak explains how to continue. Nothing is ever charged automatically for a wallet that has never subscribed.

Privacy and what Oak records

Nothing goes to HEY Research Lab unless you ask; automatic builder context on approval forms (OAK_HEY_AUTO_CONTEXT) is off by default. Oak's server keeps a code ledger (project, official X handle, checker, check date, code digest, wallet and expiry, never the raw code) until 2027-03-01. See the privacy policy.

Builder evidence, not a trading signal. HEY Research Lab tracks builder activity only. It is not an endorsement, audit, rating or buy-or-sell recommendation.

Questions? Message @OakAgentKit on X, send feedback to support@oakagentkit.org, or open an issue on GitHub.

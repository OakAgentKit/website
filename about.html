About

One MCP for the whole chain.

Meet Oak ↗

Oak simplifies access to Robinhood Chain for people and builders. Connect one assistant to the projects, tools and information you want to use.

What Oak Is, and Why

Read details

Every project on Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663) has its own contracts, its own API or subgraph, its own decimal and approval rules, and its own failure modes. Nobody wants to install and maintain 25 separate MCP servers or give 25 tools access to a wallet.

One MCP for the whole chain. Oak is one downloadable tool (@oakagentkit/addon) that connects an AI agent (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Cursor, or Grok) to 25 ecosystem projects behind one local wallet, one <module>_<verb> naming convention, one decimal base-unit amount format, pre-sign simulation previews (eth_simulateV1), and plain-sentence error messages.

Oak is built around four principles:

  1. Your keys stay on your computer. The local add-on creates the wallet automatically on first use; optional recovery and export stay in your own terminal. Your private key never leaves your computer, and Oak cannot recover a lost key. The Oak server never sees your key and never signs or sends a transaction: your swaps and deposits go straight from your wallet to the project, and subscription payments go to the burner (OakBurner) and the single-owner treasury wallet.
  2. Normalised modules, not thin wrappers. Each project module absorbs protocol-specific quirks (Uniswap v4 Permit2 expirations, Meridian multi-turn WebSocket RFQ auctions and USDe collateral swaps, TickerSpring unopened-market startingPrice bundles, Clanker MEV-window fee syncs, HoodLock proof lookups, and Merkl cumulative claim construction) so the agent interacts with clean, uniform verbs.
  3. Access only, no hidden middlemen. Oak charges a flat monthly subscription in USDG (9 USDG per 30 days on the holder plan, or 9 USDG plus one sixth of the entry price on the no-hold plan, with a 12.333333 USDG floor) and takes 0% of your trades or protocol interactions. Oak adds no safety checks, spending caps, or allowlists; you and your agent review every preview before signing.
  4. Public docs and contracts. Docs, examples and contracts are MIT. The compiled npm packages are Free-to-Use, No Copying; their source and the hosted Oak service are private.

Treasury authority

Read details

The treasury and contract admin is the single-owner wallet 0x856bb126188bDB5e425D3ac9D59E9ffBD12e6ECf. One owner key can spend treasury funds, redirect future subscription payments, replace the price signer and retire subscriptions. There is no second-signature requirement. Key compromise exposes these powers; key loss can make treasury funds and administration inaccessible. It cannot spend user wallet balances. Safe support is an optional deployment profile, not the launch setup.

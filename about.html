About Oak

At launch, Oak will let any AI agent use Robinhood Chain.

Why Oak exists

Each Robinhood Chain project has its own contracts, its own API and its own rules. For an AI agent, and for the person running it, learning 25 of them is a lot of work.

At launch, Oak will do that work once. It will be one install that gives the agent a wallet and 25 modules, and keep the naming, the amounts and the previews the same across all of them.

Oak never trades and never holds your money. The agent's own wallet makes each trade, directly on the project it picked.

Who it is for

Anyone who runs an AI agent and wants it to act on Robinhood Chain, whether or not they have used crypto before. You need a computer where the agent runs, and some funds to put in the agent's wallet.

Open and closed

At launch the local add-on, the shared core and the 25 module definitions are open source under the MIT licence. The hosted Oak server, which reads chain and project data, is a closed service.

02 · How Oak is funded

A small fee, and partner referral fees.

Oak takes a 0.25% fee on swaps and bridges. The summary shows it before you say yes, and it is included in the amount you see. Some partners also pay Oak a referral fee when you use them through Oak.

Partner referral fees

Borro is an official Oak partner. Borro's own team runs its product and contracts; Oak didn't build them and doesn't run them. Borro keeps 10% of the interest as its fee. On deposits using Oak's code, Borro pays Oak all of that fee for the first 12 months, then half. Borro pays monthly in USDG.

On hood.ag, Oak receives 5% of a name registration price and 4% of a resale price, and you get 5% or 4% off. A purchase with no referral says so in the summary.

03 · Income

25% buys back and burns $OAK.

25% of Oak's income buys back and burns $OAK. Holding $OAK has no access perks: its one link to Oak is the burns.

How a burn works ↗

What you still pay.

Network fees go to the chain, not to Oak. Project fees go to the project. Oak's own fee is the 0.25% on swaps and bridges, and it appears in the summary.

USDG is a dollar-denominated token. Oak makes no promises about token prices or investment outcomes.

Pricing

Compare plans

What it costs and when it opens

Packages are published. Hosted actions open with the token and subscription launch; until then, actions are limited to the Oak team. Read calls such as find_token remain available.

USDG is a dollar-denominated token used to pay the subscription. The entry price sets how much $OAK the holder plan requires, or the extra charge for no-hold. It is not the price of one token.

01 · 30-day access

Two ways to subscribe.

Pay 9 USDG per 30 days and hold the entry amount of $OAK; a one-time sign-up burn burns 5% of that holding. No-hold costs 9 USDG plus one sixth of the entry price, re-priced at each renewal.

Holder
9 USDG / 30 days
No-hold
12.33 USDG / 30 days*

*At the $20 entry floor: 12.333333 USDG exactly.

Holding and renewal details

Hold 20,000: 1,000 burns, and 19,000 is tracked as the required holding. Renewals burn nothing.

No $OAK holding is required on the no-hold plan.

02 · The entry curve

Scaled by market cap.

The entry price runs from $20 to $300. Pricing assumes USDG trades at a dollar.

Entry price by $OAK market cap
$OAK market cap Entry amount (hold this much $OAK before the sign-up burn) No-hold plan, per 30 days
under $100k $20 flat $12.33
$100k to $500k rises in a straight line $20 to $75 $12.33 to $21.50
$500k to $1M rises in a straight line $75 to $100 $21.50 to $25.67

over $10M $300 flat (top) $59

Full token mechanics and address notice ↗

Access follows the launch.

Curve · launch
On the Pons curve, only the no-hold plan is open at 12.333333 USDG; the holder plan is closed.
Warmup · wait
When the token moves from its launch curve into a trading pool (graduation), both plans wait for 30 minutes of pool history.
Ready · both plans
Once the pool meets the history, depth and trade-count thresholds, the holder plan is open.

Payments and renewals

While Oak is running, an active subscription renews when three days or fewer remain, using available USDG and local signing. Confirm the recurring cost before subscribing.

25% goes to the burn contract and 75% goes to the single-owner treasury wallet. Prepay is capped at 90 days ahead; cancelling ends access immediately and forfeits paid days.

Zero per-call fees and 0% on swaps; project fees and network gas still apply.

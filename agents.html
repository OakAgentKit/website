Tracker

Positions, theses and results

Every open position shows its value, its cost, its P/L and its thesis. Closed positions show what was realized, wins and losses both. P/L is value now plus withdrawals minus deposits, with gas and fees counted. Anything the tracker cannot read is shown as Unknown, never as zero, and its gaps are listed at the end.

Reading the tracker from Oak's server.

Check a thesis yourself

Each thesis is stamped on-chain as a metadata entry on agent #21449. The chain holds only the hash. Take the thesis text exactly as shown, with no added line break, and compute its keccak256 hash over the UTF-8 bytes. It must equal the hash shown. Then open the stamp transaction and confirm that it wrote the same hash under the metadata key shown, on agent #21449.

A thesis marked Written after entry was stamped after the position was already open. A thesis marked Written before entry was stamped with the trade or earlier. Oak does not edit a thesis once it is stamped.

Oak's tracker is a record of what Agent 1 did. It is not advice, a forecast or a promise.

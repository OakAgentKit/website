How it works

Your agent, your wallet, the whole chain.

Explore setup ↗

01 · Your computer

A wallet that stays with you.

Create it in your own terminal, never through the agent. Your private key never leaves your computer; Oak cannot recover a lost key.

02 · The Oak server

Unsigned, until your wallet signs.

The server never sees your key and never signs or sends a transaction. Inspect the preview, then sign locally.

03 · Robinhood Chain

Straight to the project.

Your swaps and deposits go straight from your wallet to the project; subscription payments go to the burner and the single-owner treasury wallet. Simulation excludes gas and cannot prove off-chain settlement.

One MCP for the whole chain.

25 ecosystem projects share one local wallet. The MIT open kit uses a closed Oak server.

Ready your agent.

At launch, create a wallet, fund it, verify or link an ERC-8004 identity, then subscribe and connect your agent. Oak adds no safety checks, allowlists or spending caps.
